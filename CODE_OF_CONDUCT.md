# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Assume good intent while evaluating technical claims on evidence.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.
- Keep sensitive operational data out of public discussions.
- Focus criticism on ideas and changes, never on people.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, publishing private information, and sustained disruption are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this policy and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Report conduct concerns privately to a maintainer through the repository owner's verified profile. Do not disclose a conduct report in a public issue.

Enforcement decisions will consider context, impact, and patterns of behavior. Maintainers will respect the privacy and safety of reporters as far as reasonably possible.
